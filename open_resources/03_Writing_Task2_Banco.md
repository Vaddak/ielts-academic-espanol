## 1. Opinion · *To what extent do you agree or disagree?*
1. Some people believe that university education should be free for every student, regardless of their family income. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
2. Social media companies should be legally responsible for the false information that is shared on their platforms. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
3. Cities should ban private cars from their historic centres. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

**Estructura:** postura clara en la intro (*I largely agree…*) → Body 1 y 2 con un argumento cada uno + ejemplo → conclusión con **la misma intensidad**.

## 2. Discussion · *Discuss both views and give your own opinion.*
4. Some people think that children should start learning a foreign language at primary school, while others believe it is better to wait until secondary school. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
5. Some argue that governments should invest in space exploration, while others believe the money should be spent on problems on Earth. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
6. Some people believe that working from home benefits both employees and companies, while others think people work better together in an office. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Estructura:** Body 1 = vista A · Body 2 = vista B (la tuya, con más fuerza) · opinión en intro **y** conclusión.

## 3. Advantages and disadvantages · *Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?*
7. More and more people are choosing to live alone. Do the advantages of this trend outweigh the disadvantages?
8. Many museums now allow visitors to explore their collections online. Do the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages?
9. In many countries, people are retiring later than in the past. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

**Estructura:** Body 1 = el lado que pierde · Body 2 = el lado que gana · veredicto explícito (*On balance, the advantages clearly outweigh…*).

## 4. Problems and solutions · *What problems…? What measures…?*
10. In many cities, young people cannot afford to buy or rent a home. What problems does this cause, and what measures could be taken to solve them?
11. Food waste is increasing in many developed countries. What are the causes of this problem, and how can it be reduced?
12. Many people today spend most of their working day sitting down. What problems can this cause, and what solutions can you suggest?

**Estructura:** Body 1 = 2 problemas o causas · Body 2 = una solución **para cada uno** (conectarlos).

## 5. Direct (two-part) question · dos preguntas seguidas
13. Many people say they care about the environment, yet few change their daily habits. Why is this the case? What can be done to encourage greener behaviour?
14. Fewer people today write letters or postcards by hand. Why has this happened? Is this a positive or negative development?
15. Many young people today want to become famous. What are the reasons for this? Is it a good ambition to have?

**Estructura:** **un body por pregunta**, y en la intro anunciar las dos respuestas.

## ✅ Checklist antes de entregar (errores típicos de hispanohablantes)
- ☐ Postura **igual** en la intro y en la conclusión
- ☐ ***Although,*** con coma no existe → ***However,*** (o *Although X, Y* en la misma frase)
- ☐ Incontables sin *a* ni *-s*: **research, advice, information, accommodation, evidence**
- ☐ Sin *the* con nombres generales: *~~the~~ technology changes ~~the~~ society*
- ☐ Falsos amigos: ~~present~~ (= presentar un aumento) → *show / experience* · ~~expose~~ → *explain* · ~~actual~~ → *current* · ~~use to~~ → *usually*
- ☐ Preposiciones: *invest **in** · spend **on** · related **to** · depend **on***
- ☐ **Sujeto** en cada verbo (*~~is important~~* → ***it** is important*)
- ☐ Releer **la frase entera** después de corregir algo
